Scoring Rules

Outwit

  • +2 — attended tribal council and received zero votes
  • +3 — votes nullified by an idol play

Outplay

  • +2 — won individual immunity
  • +1 — on team/tribe that won a challenge (reward or immunity)
  • +1 — won individual reward

Outlast

  • +1 — remained in the game after a tribal council
  • Finale bonus: +1 third place, +2 runner-up, +3 Sole Survivor

Tiebreaker: correct Sole Survivor pick.
